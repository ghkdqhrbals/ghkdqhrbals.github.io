Zonwake support

Choose a city, date, and time. Your iPhone calculates when the alarm will ring.

Which time zone does an alarm use?

The selected city's time zone is the reference. Traveling or changing your phone's time zone does not change that city. The alarm detail shows both the city time and your time.

How do I edit or delete an alarm?

Tap an alarm in the list to see its details. Use the edit button there, or swipe an alarm in the list to edit or delete it.

How far ahead are repeating alarms scheduled?

The app aims to schedule about 30 days ahead in the selected city's time zone and replenishes reservations when opened. If the app remains unopened for a long time, later occurrences may not be reserved. Check the scheduling coverage in the alarm details.

An alarm did not ring, or my ad-removal purchase is missing.

For alarms, check Zonwake's alarm permission in iOS Settings and the app's scheduling status. For purchases, tap “Restore purchases” on the “Remove ads” screen or check the subscription in your Apple Account.
