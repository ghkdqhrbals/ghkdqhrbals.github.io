Zonwake

Privacy policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Zonwake is an iPhone app that sets alarms in a chosen city's time zone. Its alarm features require no account or location permission.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your alarm city, date, time, repeat days, title, notes, sound, snooze setting, and scheduling state on your device. It also stores your app-language preference. It uses iOS AlarmKit to schedule and display alarms. The app operator does not send alarm content or notes to its own server or access them remotely.

Ads and consent

A Google AdMob banner may appear on the home and new-alarm screens for free users. Where applicable, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for advertising privacy choices. Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt may appear before the app requests a live ad. You can decline and still use the alarms; ad requests remain non-personalized in either case. If you decline, the Google ads SDK does not send the advertising identifier (IDFA) with ad requests. Non-personalized ads may still involve Google processing IP address, device information, advertising-related identifiers, and diagnostics for delivery, frequency capping, and measurement. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where available, you can reopen your choices from “Ad privacy” in the app's settings.

Subscriptions and payment

Apple processes payment for the monthly ad-removal subscription. RevenueCat checks subscription status and restores purchases. It may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase history and receipts, and device or technical information. The app operator does not receive your payment-card information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and Apple privacy policy.

Retention and choices

You can delete alarms in the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; any copy in an Apple device backup follows Apple's backup settings. Google and RevenueCat retain and delete information under their own policies. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your iPhone's Apple Account subscription settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Zonwake support page. If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and content here.
